Ubuntu Touch Installer

for the Xiaomi Pad 5 (nabu) · runs entirely in your browser, nothing is uploaded

This browser cannot talk to USB devices

Please open this page with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS). Firefox and Safari are not supported.

Or use the classic installer in this folder: install-windows.bat, install-macos.command, install-linux.sh.

1Before you start

  1. The tablet's bootloader must be unlocked (Xiaomi's Mi Unlock tool).
  2. The tablet must run a stock HyperOS or MIUI 13/14 (tested base: HyperOS OS1.0.6.0.TKXEUXM). Coming from a custom ROM or unsure? Open installer-any-rom.html instead: it explains every case.
  3. Charge the battery above 50% and use a good USB cable.
Everything on the tablet will be erased — photos, apps, accounts. Copy what you want to keep to another device first.

Good to know: this project was created by its author in cooperation with an AI assistant. It is experimental — use it at your own risk.

2Choose the installer folder

Select the folder that contains this page (the one with the images folder inside). Chrome will ask to "upload" the files: nothing leaves your computer.

3Connect the tablet

  1. Turn the tablet off.
  2. Hold Volume Down + Power until the FASTBOOT screen appears.
  3. Connect it to this computer, then press the button and pick the tablet in the list (it may be called "Android" or "Xiaomi").
The tablet is not in the list (Windows)

Windows needs a USB driver for fastboot. Install the "Android Bootloader Interface" (WinUSB) driver — for example with Google's USB driver or Zadig — then reconnect the tablet and try again.

4Install

Takes about 10–15 minutes. Do not unplug the tablet or close this page.
